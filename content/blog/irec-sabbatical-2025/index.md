Prof. Eran Edri has begun a sabbatical year at [IREC Barcelona](https://www.irec.cat), following a summer residency at UC Berkeley. The lab continues all research programs at BGU. Eran remains actively involved in all projects — contact him at [edrier@bgu.ac.il](mailto:edrier@bgu.ac.il).

The sabbatical was preceded by invited seminars at UC Berkeley, UCSB, USC, TU Munich, ICIQ Tarragona, and IREC.
